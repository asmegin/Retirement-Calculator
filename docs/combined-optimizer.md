# Combined plan optimizer

The main spending, net-worth and tax goals now run one coordinated search and show only the selected objective. The separate withdrawal-order comparison remains an optional advanced tool.

The search revisits withdrawal orders, future CPP/QPP and OAS start ages, eligible RRSP/TFSA contribution allocation and marginal-rate floors. An opt-in checkbox includes rental sale years and future CCA. Annual withdrawal targets are also tested before returning one verified configuration. Already-started benefits remain fixed. Applying a spending recommendation saves its recommended monthly amount as the spending goal, visible on Overview. Category spending schedules are scaled proportionally so the saved plan reproduces the recommendation. Account contribution amounts are retained. Tax and net-worth objectives retain the entered spending goal.

**Retirement age and monthly spending** compares retiring together in the same calendar year at five-year intervals, starting at the first person's age 50 and including their planning lifespan endpoint. Each row runs the combined spending optimizer independently with its own retirement ages, using the rental checkbox when selected. Past years and dates beyond a household member's configured estate lifespan are unavailable. Each scenario assumes working until the displayed ages; later retirement leaves fewer retirement years. Choose a row and apply it to save both retirement ages, spending and optimized settings. The search ceiling of $40,000/month is identified when reached.

This is a bounded coordinate search, not an exhaustive search of every joint combination. Spending candidates are screened within a $50/month bracket; finalists and the returned capacity use full precision. Rental dates are sampled at ten-year intervals and refined around promising dates. Results remain proposals until Apply and save, which checks for changes to the saved plan before writing.
