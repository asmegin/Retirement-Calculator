# v1.0.7

Released September 13, 2026.

This release corrects two tax calculations. Projected tax, spending capacity and
net worth will change for affected households, so re-check any plan you rely on.

## Tax corrections

- **OAS recovery tax is no longer counted twice.** The clawback was added on top
  of income tax calculated on the full taxable income. The Canada Revenue Agency
  assesses the repayment on net income before it (line 23400), then deducts it
  (line 23500) in arriving at net income and taxable income. Income tax, the age
  amount reduction, the federal basic personal amount phase-out and the Ontario
  health premium now all apply to income net of the repayment. Fully repaid OAS
  is tax neutral, as it should be. A single Ontario household with a large RRSP
  and fifteen clawback years pays $64,795 less projected lifetime tax, and
  marginal rates inside the clawback band fall by roughly six percentage points.
- **Ontario health premium top tier.** The final $150 step started at $200,600
  instead of $200,000, understating the premium by up to $150 for taxable income
  between $200,000 and $201,200.

## Market risk

- Monte Carlo percentile bands and the median and worst-case final values were
  always in future dollars while every other figure on Overview followed the
  today's-dollars setting. They now follow it too, and the chart redraws when the
  setting changes instead of leaving the previous units on screen.

## Plan optimizer

- **Repeat runs no longer always claim an improvement.** The current plan was
  measured with its saved annual withdrawal schedule while every candidate had
  one removed, so re-optimizing an already optimized plan reported a gain that
  was not there. The search now starts from the same footing as its candidates.
- **Candidates are no longer measured more roughly than the plan they replace.**
  The incumbent was scored at full precision against candidates using a $50
  tolerance, which hid small genuine improvements. Both now use the same
  precision while searching, and finalists are still verified at full precision.
- **Progress reflects the real amount of work.** Verification and the annual
  withdrawal schedule were excluded from the evaluation count, so a 32-second run
  reported 58 evaluations. Both are now counted and progress runs against the
  total budget. Results say when the search used its whole budget.
- **Results show what reaching the goal cost.** Lifetime tax, final net worth and
  lifetime benefits changes now appear for every goal, so a plan that reaches its
  target by giving up Guaranteed Income Supplement or estate value is visible.
  For the spending goal these compare against the current plan at its own
  capacity rather than at its lower spending goal.
- **Retirement age options stop at 75.** The sweep ran to the planning lifespan
  and offered retiring at 85 or 90, which saturated the spending ceiling and
  produced identical rows. It now runs from the first age not already past up to
  75, which also makes the search noticeably faster.
- **Withdrawal order comparison no longer lists the current strategy twice** when
  no annual schedule is saved, removing a duplicate row and its spending solve.

## Interface

- Checkboxes were 13 pixels, below the 24 pixel minimum target size; they are now
  18 pixels, and 24 pixels on the narrow layout.
- A planning lifespan that ends before the plan starts reported a fully funded
  plan made entirely of zeroes. It now explains that there are no years to
  project and points at the settings to change.
- Normalized straight apostrophes in interface text.

## Validation

- Added engine coverage for the OAS recovery deduction and for the Ontario health
  premium across all twenty-one statutory tier boundaries.
- Added optimizer coverage for the reported side effects, evaluation budget and
  the bounded retirement age sweep.
- Node suite is now 90 tests, up from 86. All three browser suites pass.

## Upgrade

- **Docker/Unraid:** update to `ghcr.io/asmegin/retirement-calculator:1.0.7`, keep
  the existing `/app/data` mapping, and recreate the container.
- **Compose:** set `APP_VERSION=1.0.7`, then run `docker compose pull` and
  `docker compose up -d --no-build`.
- **Standalone:** download `retirement-calculator-standalone.zip` from the v1.0.7
  release and replace the old application files. Export a plan backup before
  upgrading.
- No plan data changes are required. Saved plans load unchanged; only the
  calculated results move.
