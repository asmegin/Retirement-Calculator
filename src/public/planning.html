03 / MAKE YOUR SAVINGS WORK

A withdrawal plan, year by year

Compare withdrawal orders, then search annual RRSP/RRIF dollar targets across your full retirement. The solver respects mandatory minimums, recalculates tax and benefits, and preserves a funded baseline and its ending estate when reducing tax.

Choose an objective to start. This is a bounded numerical search, not a guarantee of the global minimum tax.
04 / GOVERNMENT PENSIONS

Build from your earnings history

Use your statement estimate, or import pensionable earnings to model the base pension, general dropout, child-rearing periods and enhanced components. Annual rows are spread evenly across months. Monthly records improve precision; future earnings and special-case benefit adjudication remain estimates.

CSV columns: year,month,earnings,caregiver,disabled. Omit month for annual earnings. Optional ympe,yampe override published annual ceilings. Earnings must be unformatted numbers; caregiver/disabled accept true or false. Flag only eligible primary-caregiver periods for children under 7. Credits shared after separation, post-retirement benefits, CPP disability enhancement drop-ins and QPP special-care extensions require government estimates.
05 / THE PEOPLE YOU CARE ABOUT

What passes to your family?

Choose each person’s lifespan and model year-end death, spousal rollovers, survivor spending, deemed dispositions, terminal registered-account income and probate. Pension survivor percentages can be edited below.

Probate presets: Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta and a Quebec notarial will. Enter an override for other provinces or wills. Named-beneficiary exclusions are configurable per account. CPP/QPP survivor benefits and complex corporate post-mortem planning are not automatically assumed.

06 / YOUR BUSINESS, YOUR RETIREMENT

Corporate savings and distributions

Track a corporation’s investment pool, CDA, eligible/non-eligible refundable tax balances and GRIP. Optional business profit flows through salary, corporate tax and dividends into retained savings.

Use one operating corporation per person. Business profit is before owner salary and employer CPP, after other operating expenses. Effective tax rates are editable and must match the corporation’s province and circumstances. Associated passive income reduces the federal small-business limit; provincial rules can differ. CDA elections, loss carryforwards and full T2 filing are outside this projection.

07 / PUT EXTRA CASH TO WORK

Pay down debt or invest?

Add annual mortgage prepayments, compare the same annual budget invested, or model readvancing repaid mortgage principal into a dedicated taxable investment account.

Smith-style borrowing models a separate interest-only investment loan with a borrowing limit. Deductibility assumes borrowed dollars are traced directly to investments expected to earn taxable income; it does not make personal or registered-account borrowing deductible. Interest, assets and outstanding debt all remain in the projection. Lender restrictions and Quebec investment-expense limits require separate assessment.

08 / KEEP MORE OF YOUR INCOME

Benefits alongside your savings

TFSA withdrawals do not reduce GIS. Compare a benefits-focused withdrawal search with the baseline and enter each person’s prior-year GIS income for the first projection year.

GIS and Ontario GAINS use annual planning estimates. Check residency and eligibility before enabling GAINS. Existing provincial non-refundable low-income tax reductions are included; refundable credits in other provinces are not yet modelled.
